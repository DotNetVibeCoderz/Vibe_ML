# Gravicode.MediaPipeNet.Models.FaceStylizer

The BlazeFaceStylizer "color sketch" generator (256×256) and the 192×192 face mesh it aligns faces with, as ONNX, copied to `models/` in your output folder. Used by `FaceStylizer` together with the face models from `Gravicode.MediaPipeNet.Models.Face`.
