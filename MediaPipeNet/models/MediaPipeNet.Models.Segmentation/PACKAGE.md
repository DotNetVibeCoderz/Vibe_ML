# Gravicode.MediaPipeNet.Models.Segmentation

Segmentation models as ONNX, copied to `models/` in your output folder: selfie segmenter, selfie multiclass (6 classes), hair segmenter, DeepLab v3 (21 classes) and the MagicTouch interactive segmenter.
