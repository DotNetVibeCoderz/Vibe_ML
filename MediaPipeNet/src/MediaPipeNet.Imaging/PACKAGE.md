# Gravicode.MediaPipeNet.Imaging

`MPImage` (pooled RGBA frames from files, streams, byte arrays, ImageSharp images or raw BGR/RGBA buffers), rotated-ROI **image-to-tensor** conversion with letterboxing, normalization and anti-aliasing, mask projection, and the `IFrameSource` abstraction for video.

---
**MediaPipe.NET** — a native .NET 10 port of Google MediaPipe's vision tasks on ONNX Runtime.
