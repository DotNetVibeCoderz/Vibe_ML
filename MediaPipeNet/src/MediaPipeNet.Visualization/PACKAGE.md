# Gravicode.MediaPipeNet.Visualization

Drawing helpers on ImageSharp images: `LandmarkDrawer`, `BoundingBoxDrawer`, `SegmentationMaskOverlay` (tint, blur background, replace background) and `ResultRenderer.Render(image, result)` for every result type.

---
**MediaPipe.NET** — a native .NET 10 port of Google MediaPipe's vision tasks on ONNX Runtime.
